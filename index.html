Current project

Whiteboards for All

In many Tanzanian classrooms, teachers still write on blackboards with chalk, and pupils work on small chalk slates at their desks. That means chalk dust in the air that students and teachers breathe all day, every day.

Whiteboards for All replaces blackboards and slates with clean, long-lasting whiteboards and markers, so the classroom is a healthier place to spend the day.

  • No chalk dust in the air or on hands and uniforms
  • Sharper, easier-to-read writing for every row of the class
  • Personal whiteboards so every pupil can practise and show their work

How it works

From European partners to Tanzanian classrooms.

Our partners and donors in Europe help equip schools in Tanzania. We handle the practical side so that every donation arrives safely and gets used.

Partners give

Donors, companies and organisations in Europe support us with funding or with equipment we actually need.

We source and ship

We source equipment in Europe, pack it carefully and organise shipping to Tanzania.

Customs and delivery

We handle the paperwork and customs clearance, then deliver directly to the schools we work with.

In the classroom

Equipment goes into daily use, and we follow up with schools to make sure it's making a difference.
