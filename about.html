About TASPO

We believe every student deserves a fair start.

TASPO, the Tanzania Student Plan Organization, is a non-governmental organisation working with schools in Tanzania to improve the conditions students learn in and the skills they learn with.

Our story

Why TASPO exists

TASPO was founded in [YEAR] by [FOUNDER NAME(S)] out of a simple observation: Tanzanian students are ambitious and capable, yet too many are held back by the basics, such as dusty classrooms, too little equipment and few chances to learn how to organise their own studies.

We started with what was right in front of us: the classroom itself. Today our work covers healthier classroom equipment, student planning tools and clubs that give students room to grow beyond the timetable.

We work closely with head teachers, teachers and local authorities in Tanzania, and with partners and donors across Europe who help make it all possible.

Our mission

To give Tanzanian students healthier, better-equipped classrooms and the skills to plan and take charge of their own learning.

Our vision

A Tanzania where every student learns in a classroom that supports them and leaves school ready to shape their own future.

Our values

How we work

Practical

We focus on things that make a real difference in the classroom tomorrow, not in a report next year.

Transparent

Our partners and donors should always know where their support goes and what it achieved.

Respectful

We work with schools and communities, never over them. Local knowledge leads the way.

Long-term

We choose durable equipment and lasting habits over quick fixes that fade.
