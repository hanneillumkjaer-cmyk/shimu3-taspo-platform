Get involved

Help us equip the next classroom.

There are many ways to support TASPO's work. Whatever you choose, you'll know exactly where your help goes.

Make a donation

Financial donations help us buy whiteboards and school equipment, cover shipping and customs, and run our clubs. We'll gladly tell you what your gift made possible.

To donate, please contact us and we'll send you our bank details and any information you need for your records.

Partner with us

We welcome partnerships with companies, foundations, schools and organisations in Europe and Tanzania. You could sponsor a school, fund a whole Whiteboards for All classroom or support the TASPO Taskbook.

Donate equipment

We coordinate equipment donations from Europe to Tanzania. We're especially interested in:

  • Whiteboards (classroom and mini size)
  • Whiteboard markers, erasers and cleaning supplies
  • School stationery and teaching materials
  • IT equipment in good working order

Please contact us before sending anything, so we can check that it's needed and plan shipping and customs.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where does my donation go?

Your donation goes to our programmes in Tanzania: equipment such as whiteboards, the shipping and customs costs needed to get it there, and our after-school clubs. If you'd like your gift to go to a particular programme, just tell us.

Can I donate equipment from anywhere in Europe?

Yes. We coordinate donations from across Europe. Please contact us first with details of what you'd like to donate, and we'll let you know whether it's needed and how to get it to us.

Can my company sponsor a specific school?

Yes. We can link your support to a specific school or classroom and keep you updated on how it's being used. Get in touch to talk about the options.

Is TASPO a registered organisation?

Yes. TASPO is a registered non-governmental organisation in Tanzania (registration no. ooNGO/1910). Contact us if you need documentation for your records.

How will I know what my support achieved?

We keep our partners and donors updated with news from the schools we work with, and we share regular updates on our blog.
