Donate now

Help give every student a fair start.

Your donation helps Tanzanian students learn in healthier, better-equipped classrooms. Every gift, large or small, goes to our programmes in Tanzania.

Where your donation goes

  • Whiteboards for classrooms. Whiteboards, mini whiteboards and the markers and erasers to keep them going, replacing chalk and blackboards through Whiteboards for All.
  • Getting equipment there. The shipping and customs costs needed to get donated equipment safely into the schools we work with.
  • Learning beyond the timetable. Our academic and socio-economic after-school clubs, and the TASPO Taskbook.

If you'd like your gift to go to a particular programme or school, just tell us and we'll make sure it does. We keep our donors updated with news from the schools we work with.

How to donate

Donations are made by bank transfer directly to TASPO's bank account in Tanzania. Email us and we'll send you the account details, confirm when your donation arrives, and let you know what it made possible.

Email us to donate
